Overview

Led the implementation of Salesforce B2B Order Management to streamline order processing and integrate Salesforce CPQ with the client's e-commerce platform, improving order accuracy, inventory management and fulfillment for direct and channel sales.

Key Responsibilities and Achievements
  • Gathered business requirements with stakeholders for integrating Salesforce CPQ with the existing e-commerce platform.
  • Designed and documented the integration architecture that automates the flow of orders from Salesforce CPQ and the e-commerce site into B2B OMS.
  • Configured Salesforce CPQ to automate pricing, quoting, and proposal generation in line with customer-specific pricing rules and discounts.
  • Defined and implemented OMS order workflows for routing, approvals, and fulfillment based on business rules and SLAs.
  • Ran unit, integration, and user acceptance testing, managed deployment, and provided post-deployment support.
  • Developed training materials and ran workshops for end users and administrators.
  • Monitored system performance and ran regular audits to keep the solution scalable and efficient.
